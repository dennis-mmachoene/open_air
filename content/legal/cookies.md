This Cookie Policy explains how Open Air (operated by Dennis Ramara) uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. "Local storage" is a related browser technology that stores small amounts of data on your device. Both let a site remember information between page loads and visits. We group these technologies into **strictly necessary**, **functional**, and **analytics** categories.

## 2. How we use them

We keep our use of cookies deliberately minimal. The catalog can be browsed without signing in, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

### 2.1 Strictly necessary
These are required for the Service to function and cannot be switched off through our interface.

| Name / purpose | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication session | Cookie | Set when you sign in, to keep you logged in and secure your session. |
| Security / CSRF | Cookie | Helps protect authenticated requests against forgery. |

### 2.2 Functional
These remember your preferences and improve your experience.

| Name / purpose | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Theme preference | Local storage | Remembers light/dark and similar interface preferences. |
| `oa_recent` (recently viewed) | Cookie | Stores the slugs of palettes you recently viewed so we can show them on your dashboard. |
| Onboarding / UI state | Local storage | Remembers small interface choices, such as filters. |

### 2.3 Analytics
These help us understand how the Service is used so we can improve it. They are only active when analytics is enabled for the deployment.

| Provider | Purpose | Description |
|---|---|---|
| PostHog | Product analytics | Measures page views and feature usage to improve the product. Configured to identify users only where appropriate. |
| Sentry | Error monitoring | Helps diagnose errors and performance issues (where enabled). |

## 3. Your choices

- **Browser controls.** You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in or using account features.
- **Analytics opt-out.** Where required by law, non-essential analytics are used only with your consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time. You can also use your browser's "Do Not Track" or global privacy control signals; we honour recognised opt-out signals where applicable.
- **Local storage.** You can clear local storage through your browser's site-data controls.

## 4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by the third-party providers we use (for example, Google for authentication and the analytics providers above). Their use of cookies is governed by their own policies. See the [Data Processing & Security Statement](/legal/data-processing) for the list of providers.

## 5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of these technologies evolves. We will revise the "Effective" date when we do.
