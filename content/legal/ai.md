This AI Usage & Disclaimer explains how Open Air's AI features work, how your data is handled, and the limits of AI output. It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms) and should be read with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and [Disclaimer](/legal/disclaimer).

## 1. What the AI feature does

Open Air offers an AI **colour concierge** ("AI Assistant") that helps you find and reason about palettes in natural language. When you submit a prompt, the Assistant may suggest palettes from the catalog and provide explanatory text.

## 2. How it works

2.1 **Third-party model.** When configured, the Assistant sends the text of your prompt to a third-party large-language-model provider (Google, via the Gemini API) to generate a response. Your prompt and the model's response are processed by that provider under its terms.

2.2 **Local fallback.** If the AI provider is not configured or is unavailable, the Assistant falls back to local keyword matching that runs without sending your prompt to an external model.

2.3 **Rate limits and budgets.** To protect the Service and manage cost, AI requests are rate-limited per user and subject to an overall daily ceiling. Requests may be declined when limits are reached.

## 3. Your data and prompts

- **Do not submit sensitive personal information** (such as identity numbers, health information, or financial details) in AI prompts.
- Prompts you submit may be processed by the third-party provider to generate responses. We do not use your prompts to train our own models.
- See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for how we handle the data associated with AI use.

## 4. Nature and limits of AI output

4.1 **Assistance, not advice.** AI output is provided to assist your own judgement. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for your specific needs.

4.2 **No guarantee of accessibility or fitness.** While Open Air's catalog is curated with accessibility in mind, AI-generated suggestions and explanations are **not** a substitute for testing colours in your real context. Always verify contrast and accessibility for your specific use case.

4.3 **No professional advice.** AI output is not legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice.

4.4 **Your responsibility.** You are responsible for reviewing, verifying, and deciding whether to rely on any AI output, and for your use of it.

## 5. Acceptable use of AI

You must not use the AI Assistant to generate unlawful, harmful, hateful, deceptive, or infringing content, to attempt to extract other users' data, or to manipulate the model into violating our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).
