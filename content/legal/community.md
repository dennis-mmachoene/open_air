These Community & Content Guidelines describe the standards for content you create and store on Open Air (operated by Open Air). They form part of our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms) and complement the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 1. Scope

Open Air currently lets you create private content within your own account: **saved palettes**, **generated palettes**, **collections**, and the **names** you give them, as well as **prompts** you send to the AI assistant. This content is private to your account and is not published to other users or made publicly searchable by us. These Guidelines also anticipate future features that may allow public sharing or community contributions; if and when those launch, these Guidelines will apply to them.

## 2. Content standards

Whether content is private or, in the future, shared, you agree not to create, store, name, or submit content that:

- Is unlawful, or promotes illegal activity.
- Infringes the intellectual property, privacy, publicity, or other rights of any person.
- Is defamatory, harassing, threatening, hateful, or discriminatory on the basis of a protected characteristic.
- Is sexually explicit, or sexualises or endangers minors in any way.
- Contains malware, or is designed to deceive, phish, or defraud.
- Impersonates another person or misrepresents your identity or affiliation.
- Reveals another person's private or sensitive information without authorisation.

## 3. Naming

When naming palettes or collections, do not use names that are unlawful, infringing, hateful, or that impersonate a brand or person in a misleading way.

## 4. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the content you create and store. You represent that you have the rights necessary to use it and that it complies with these Guidelines and applicable law.

## 5. Moderation and enforcement

Because most content is private, we do not routinely monitor it. However, we may review content where necessary to operate the Service, comply with law, respond to a report, or protect users and the public. We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these Guidelines, the AUP, or the law. For copyright concerns specifically, see the [Copyright Infringement (DMCA) Policy](/legal/dmca).

## 6. Reporting

If you become aware of content or conduct that violates these Guidelines, email **openair.mailer@gmail.com**.

## 7. Changes

We may update these Guidelines as the Service evolves, particularly if we introduce public sharing or collaboration features.
