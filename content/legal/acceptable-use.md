This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out what you may and may not do when using Open Air (operated by Dennis Ramara). It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms). Breaching this AUP may lead to suspension or termination of your Account.

## 1. General principle

Use the Service lawfully, respectfully, and in a way that does not harm Open Air, its infrastructure, or other users. The Service is a colour-exploration tool; use it for that purpose and related legitimate creative, design, and development work.

## 2. Prohibited activities

You must not:

### 2.1 Misuse the platform
- Access, tamper with, or use non-public areas of the Service, our systems, or our providers' systems without authorisation.
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service, or breach or circumvent any security or authentication measure.
- Circumvent, disable, or interfere with usage limits, rate limits, entitlement checks, or access controls (including Free/Pro gating).

### 2.2 Overload or disrupt
- Use bots, scrapers, or automated means to harvest the catalog or place unreasonable load on the Service, except through the public API within its documented limits.
- Introduce malware, launch denial-of-service attacks, or otherwise disrupt the integrity or performance of the Service.

### 2.3 Abuse the API
- Exceed published rate limits, share or resell API keys, or use the API to build a substantially similar competing catalog.
- Misrepresent the source of API data or remove attribution where required.

### 2.4 Abuse the AI assistant
- Submit prompts designed to extract another user's data, generate unlawful content, or manipulate the model into violating this AUP.
- Use the assistant to generate spam, malware, or harassing, hateful, or unlawful content.

### 2.5 Violate rights or laws
- Infringe intellectual property, privacy, or other rights of any person.
- Use the Service for any unlawful, fraudulent, or deceptive purpose, or to violate export-control or sanctions laws.

### 2.6 Misuse content
- Upload, store, or name content (such as collection or palette names) that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, hateful, sexually exploitative, or otherwise prohibited by the [Community & Content Guidelines](/legal/community).

## 3. Fair use of the catalog

Individual colour values are facts you may freely use in your designs. However, you may not copy, scrape, or republish the curated catalog as a whole, or substantial parts of it, including its selection, arrangement, names, and explanatory content, except as permitted by the [Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy](/legal/ip).

## 4. Security research

We welcome good-faith security research. If you discover a vulnerability, report it privately to **dennismmachoene@gmail.com** and give us a reasonable opportunity to remediate before any disclosure. Do not access or modify other users' data, degrade the Service, or exfiltrate data beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue.

## 5. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and may suspend or terminate access, remove content, or take other appropriate action. Where conduct is unlawful, we may report it to the relevant authorities.

## 6. Reporting abuse

To report misuse of the Service, email **dennismmachoene@gmail.com**.
