This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how to cancel an Open Air subscription and when refunds apply. It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms) and [Subscription & Billing Terms](/legal/billing).

## 1. Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel at any time from the self-service billing portal in your account settings. When you cancel:

- Your subscription stops renewing.
- You keep **Pro access until the end of the billing period you have already paid for**.
- At the end of that period, your account moves to the Free plan. Your saved palettes, collections, and generated palettes are retained, subject to Free-tier limits (for example, the five-saved-palette limit applies to new saves, not retroactively to existing ones).

## 2. Refunds

2.1 **General position.** Subscriptions are billed in advance for the selected period. Because the Service is a digital product delivered immediately, we do not generally provide pro-rata refunds for the unused portion of a billing period after it has started.

2.2 **Statutory rights.** Nothing in this Policy limits rights you have under applicable consumer-protection law that cannot be excluded, including the South African Consumer Protection Act, 2008, and, where applicable, EU/UK distance-selling rights. Where the law grants you a right to a refund, we will honour it.

2.3 **Discretionary refunds.** We may, at our discretion, provide a full or partial refund — for example, for a duplicate charge, a clear billing error, or where you cancelled immediately after an unintended renewal and have not made material use of Pro features in the new period. Contact us promptly to request this.

2.4 **Annual plans.** For annual plans cancelled mid-term, the default is continued access until the end of the paid year without a pro-rata refund, subject to sections 2.2 and 2.3.

## 3. Billing errors

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, contact us within a reasonable time at **openair.mailer@gmail.com** with the relevant invoice. We will investigate and correct genuine errors, including issuing refunds where appropriate.

## 4. Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us first — we can usually resolve issues faster than a chargeback. Initiating a chargeback without contacting us may result in suspension of your Account pending resolution.

## 5. Account deletion

Deleting your Account cancels any active subscription and permanently removes your data, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). Deleting your Account does not by itself entitle you to a refund of amounts already paid, subject to section 2.

## 6. Contact

Refund and cancellation questions can be sent to **openair.mailer@gmail.com**.
