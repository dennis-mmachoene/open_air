These Subscription & Billing Terms govern paid plans on Open Air (operated by Open Air). They form part of, and should be read with, our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms) and [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/legal/refunds).

## 1. Plans

Open Air offers a **Free** plan and a paid **Pro** plan.

- **Free** includes browsing the full catalog and every palette page, the "why it works" explanation, the light/dark Showroom preview, copying HEX values, CSS and Tailwind export, and saving up to five (5) palettes.
- **Pro** includes unlimited saves and personal collections, the full Showroom, all export formats (including SCSS, JSON, Figma tokens, SVG, PNG, and ASE), the gradient studio and accessibility centre, the palette generator without upgrade prompts, and access to the public API (with a key and rate limits).

The current features and prices are shown on the [Pricing page](/pricing). We may change plan composition and pricing prospectively; changes will not reduce features you have already paid for during a current paid period without notice.

## 2. Pricing, currency, and taxes

Prices are displayed on the Pricing page and at checkout. Applicable taxes (such as VAT) may be added based on your location and are calculated at checkout by our payment processor. You are responsible for any taxes not collected by us.

## 3. Billing cycles and renewal

3.1 **Subscriptions** are offered on a monthly or annual basis, as selected at checkout. Annual plans are billed once for the year and are offered at a discount to the monthly rate.

3.2 **Automatic renewal.** Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period at the then-current price for your plan, until cancelled. By subscribing, you authorise us, through our payment processor, to charge your payment method on each renewal date.

3.3 **Price changes.** If we change the price of your plan, we will give you advance notice. Changes take effect at your next renewal; you may cancel before then if you do not agree.

## 4. Payment processing

Payments are processed by **Stripe**. By subscribing, you also agree to Stripe's terms. We do not receive or store your full card details. Your plan and entitlements are derived **server-side** from the subscription recorded by Stripe; we do not rely on the browser to determine what you have paid for.

## 5. Failed payments and dunning

If a renewal payment fails, we (through Stripe) may retry the charge and will notify you by email. If payment continues to fail, your subscription may be downgraded to Free and Pro features may become unavailable until payment succeeds. Your saved data is retained subject to Free-tier limits.

## 6. Managing your subscription

You can upgrade, downgrade, update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel at any time through the self-service billing portal accessible from your account settings, which is provided by Stripe.

## 7. Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations and refunds are governed by the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/legal/refunds). In short: you may cancel at any time and will retain Pro access until the end of the period you have already paid for; we do not generally provide pro-rata refunds for partial periods, subject to your statutory rights.

## 8. Invoices and receipts

On each successful payment, we send a receipt by email that includes a link to your hosted invoice and a downloadable PDF, generated by Stripe.

## 9. Legacy plans

A previously offered "Studio" plan has been retired and folded into Pro. Existing subscribers to legacy plans, if any, retain their access until they change or cancel their subscription.
