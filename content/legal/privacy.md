This Privacy Policy explains how Open Air, operating the Open Air platform ("Open Air", "we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Open Air website, applications, and public API (the "Service"). It is written to meet the requirements of South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 ("POPIA"), and to give equivalent rights to users protected by the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA/CPRA").

We are the **responsible party** (POPIA) and **data controller** (GDPR/UK GDPR) for the personal information described here.

## 1. Summary

- We collect the minimum personal information needed to run accounts, billing, and product features.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.
- The catalog is browsable without an account and without us collecting account data.
- Images you use in the "extract palette from image" tool are processed **in your browser** and are not uploaded to our servers.
- You can access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account information.** When you sign in with Google or an email magic link, we receive and store your email address, and (from Google) your name and profile image where available. Open Air is passwordless; we never receive or store your password.
- **User content.** Palettes you save, palettes you generate, collections you create, and the names you give them.
- **AI prompts.** Text you submit to the AI colour assistant.
- **Communications.** Messages you send us by email.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage and device data.** Through our product-analytics provider (PostHog), we collect events such as pages viewed and features used, together with technical data such as browser type, approximate location derived from IP, and device characteristics.
- **Error and performance data.** Through our error-monitoring provider (Sentry, where enabled), we collect diagnostic data when something goes wrong.
- **Cookies and local storage.** As described in our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies), including a session cookie, a theme preference, and a "recently viewed" record.

### 2.3 Information from third parties
- **Authentication providers.** Google provides your email, name, and profile image when you choose Google sign-in.
- **Payment processor.** Stripe provides limited billing metadata (such as your subscription status, plan, and a customer identifier). We do **not** receive or store your full card number; Stripe handles card data directly.

### 2.4 Information we do not collect
- We do not upload or store images you analyse in the client-side image-extraction tool.
- We do not store payment card numbers.
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

## 4. The AI Assistant

When you use the AI colour assistant, the text of your prompt is sent to our third-party AI provider (Google, via the Gemini API) to generate a response. Do not include sensitive personal information in prompts. If the AI provider is unavailable or not configured, the assistant falls back to local keyword matching that does not transmit your prompt externally. See the [AI Usage & Disclaimer](/legal/ai).

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

We share personal information only as follows:

- **Service providers (operators / sub-processors).** We use trusted providers to run the Service, including Google (authentication and AI), Stripe (payments), Neon (database hosting), Upstash (rate limiting), PostHog (analytics), Sentry (error monitoring), and an email delivery provider. They process data on our behalf under contract. A current list is in the [Data Processing & Security Statement](/legal/data-processing).
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Open Air, our users, or the public.
- **Business transfers.** If the Service is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may be transferred subject to this Policy.

We do **not** sell your personal information and do **not** "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

## 6. International Transfers

Our providers may process data outside South Africa, including in the European Union, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Where we transfer personal information across borders, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms — for POPIA, the conditions in section 72; for GDPR/UK GDPR, adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses, as applicable.

## 7. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here:

- **Account and user content:** until you delete the item or your Account.
- **Billing records:** as long as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes.
- **Webhook and security logs:** for a limited operational period.
- **Analytics and error data:** per the retention settings of the relevant provider.

When you delete your Account, we permanently remove your profile, saved palettes, collections, generated palettes, and API keys, and cancel any active subscription. Residual copies may persist briefly in routine backups before being overwritten.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Delete** your information ("right to erasure").
- **Export / port** your information in a portable format.
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time where processing is based on consent.
- **Lodge a complaint** with a regulator.

You can exercise most of these rights directly: edit your profile, export your data, or delete your Account from your account settings. For anything else, contact **openair.mailer@gmail.com**. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 10. Security

We protect personal information using technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed API keys, server-side enforcement of permissions, signature-verified payment webhooks, and least-privilege access to systems. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond appropriately to incidents. See the [Data Processing & Security Statement](/legal/data-processing).
